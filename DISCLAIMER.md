# Read this first

**Binder is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.**

It reconciles information a caregiver already has access to across portals they
are already signed in to, and it produces **questions to ask a clinician**. It
does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment, and does not tell anyone to
start, stop or change a medication. Every finding it surfaces is presented with
the evidence behind it so a human can evaluate it and take it to a professional.

**All data in this repository is fabricated.** The patient, the caregiver, the
clinicians, the practices and the pharmacy do not exist. No real person's health
information appears anywhere in this project, and the application has never been
connected to a real patient portal.

The drug interactions modelled in the fixtures are real and are cited in
`apps/*/data.js`, because a demonstration built on a fake interaction would not
be worth demonstrating. Citing them is not clinical guidance.
